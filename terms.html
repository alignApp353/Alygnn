Free job slot

Second Job Slot for free employers

Free employers may purchase one additional reusable "Second Job Slot" for $150 per month, giving that employer two total job slots while the add-on remains active. Launch, Growth, Scale, and Enterprise

As of the date above, standard paid-plan job-slot capacity is structured as follows, including the employer's permanent free slot:

  • Launch: 3 plan slots + 1 permanent free slot = 4 total job slots
  • Growth: 5 plan slots + 1 permanent free slot = 6 total job slots
  • Scale: 8 plan slots + 1 permanent free slot = 9 total job slots

Launch, Growth, and Scale may be offered on monthly and/or quarterly billing intervals as displayed on the pricing page or checkout.

Weekly Job Slot

An employer with an eligible active paid plan may purchase one temporary Weekly Job Slot for $99 for seven (7) days, adding one temporary job slot during that period. The seven-day period begins immediately after successful purchase and continues whether the slot is used or unused. Closing or deleting a job, leaving the slot empty, or reusing the slot does not pause, restart, extend, or carry over the seven-day period. Alygnn may limit employers to one active Weekly Job Slot add-on at a time and may recommend upgrading the underlying plan instead of repeatedly purchasing temporary capacity.

Job Boost

Eligible paid-plan employers may purchase a Job Boost for a selected active job. The current standard price is $15 per day where the feature is offered. A boost increases visibility or placement for the selected job during the purchased period but does not guarantee views, applications, interviews, candidates, or hires. A boost does not extend the underlying job slot or subscription.

Urgently Hiring and other plan features

Eligible Growth, Scale, or other qualifying plans may display an "Urgently Hiring" badge or similar visibility feature on eligible jobs. The badge is a presentation feature only and does not represent or guarantee a particular hiring timeline, candidate response, or outcome.
