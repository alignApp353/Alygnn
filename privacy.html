1. Overview and scope

Alygnn ("Alygnn," "we," "us," or "our") operates a job-matching and recruiting platform through alygnn.com, related web pages, and supported mobile applications (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices available to candidates, employers, and employer team members.

This policy applies when you create or use an account, upload or build a resume, set job preferences, browse or apply to jobs, communicate through Alygnn, operate an employer account, verify a business, review candidates, purchase a plan or add-on, or otherwise use the Service.

2. Information we collect

Account and authentication information

  • Name, email address, account role, and account identifiers
  • Authentication information handled through our authentication provider, including email/password credentials or supported Google or Apple sign-in tokens. Alygnn does not have access to your plaintext password.
  • Account settings, role or mode changes, verification state, and security-related account events

Candidate profile and resume information

  • Uploaded resume files and Alygnn-built resume data
  • Resume details such as contact information, professional title, professional summary, employment history, education, skills, awards, and other resume content you choose to provide
  • Which resume you select as your active resume when more than one resume is available
  • Candidate profile details such as name, headline, and other profile information you add
  • Job preferences, including desired roles, compensation, locations, work style, job type, benefits, industries, and related matching preferences

Application and activity information

  • Jobs you view, apply to, skip, save, or otherwise interact with
  • Application timestamps and status information
  • Employer actions associated with an application, such as accepted or passed/not selected status
  • Alygnn Match scores, match labels, and job-related information used to produce those informational results
  • Search terms, filters, and other actions used to operate candidate or employer workflows

Employer and business verification information

  • Company name, description, website, phone number, business email, business address, and employer account details
  • EIN or other business identifiers and verification documents or information submitted to verify the business
  • Verification status, review history, and information generated or recorded by authorized Alygnn administrators during verification
  • Job postings, including job title, description, duties, qualifications, compensation, benefits, location, work arrangement, job type, and related posting details
  • Employer team members, roles, invitations, permissions, and related access information

Recruiting, candidate-review, and messaging information

  • Candidate lists and candidate-review activity associated with an employer's jobs
  • Candidate profiles and resumes made available to an employer after an application or through another supported recruiting flow
  • Messages exchanged between candidates and employers, including message content, timestamps, conversation participants, and the associated job/application
  • Employer decisions and organization data, such as accepted and passed candidate groupings
  • Notifications such as new applicant, message, application, and account notifications

Payment, subscription, and entitlement information

When an employer purchases a paid plan, Second Job Slot, Weekly Job Slot, Job Boost, team seat, or other paid feature, payment-card information is processed by Stripe or another payment processor. Alygnn may receive and store:

  • Customer and transaction identifiers
  • Product or plan purchased, job-slot entitlement, seat entitlement, boost entitlement, billing interval, amount, currency, and payment status
  • Subscription start, renewal, cancellation, expiration, and next-billing information
  • Limited billing details made available by the payment processor
  • Payment, refund, dispute, charge failure, and webhook event information needed to operate billing

Alygnn does not store full payment-card numbers or card security codes.

Technical, device, and usage information

We may collect browser type, operating system, device type, app version, IP address, approximate geographic information derived from technical data, timestamps, error logs, security signals, and general usage information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Service.

Location and place information you provide

If you enter a city, state, address, job location, or similar place information, we process that information to provide location-based features and matching. If a supported address or place-autocomplete provider is used, the text you enter may be sent to that provider to return suggestions.

Support and contact information

We collect information you send when you contact Alygnn, request help, report abuse, dispute a transaction, request an account or name change, or otherwise communicate with us.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, secure, and maintain accounts
  • Support candidate and employer roles and account switching where available
  • Store, display, and manage uploaded resumes and Alygnn-built resumes
  • Remember the resume a candidate selected for use in the Service
  • Provide job discovery, application, skip, status, and My Jobs features
  • Provide employer job-posting, candidate-review, accepted/passed organization, and recruiting workflows
  • Calculate informational Alygnn Match results from job-related resume and posting content
  • Provide messaging between candidates and employers and associate conversations with the relevant job/application
  • Verify employer identity and business legitimacy
  • Provide employer plans, permanent and temporary job slots, billing, team seats, Job Boosts, plan badges, and other entitlements
  • Process purchases, renewals, cancellations, expirations, payment failures, refunds where applicable, and billing support
  • Send verification, password reset, application, message, candidate, billing, service, and security communications
  • Detect, investigate, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, spam, threats, misuse, and security incidents
  • Provide customer support, troubleshoot issues, measure reliability, and improve the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

4. Alygnn Match and AI-assisted processing

Alygnn Match may compare job-related information from a job posting with information in a candidate's resume, such as skills, work experience, role history, and explicitly required education, to produce an informational match score or label.

Alygnn does not intend to use protected characteristics such as race, ethnicity, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, disability, age, or other legally protected traits to calculate Alygnn Match. The score does not make a hiring decision and is not intended to be a solely automated employment decision with legal or similarly significant effect. Employers remain responsible for independently reviewing candidates and complying with employment and anti-discrimination laws.

We may also use AI-assisted services for functions such as resume parsing, draft job-description generation, matching, and reviewing custom job-posting entries for relevance, professionalism, or spam. Information sent to an AI service provider is limited to what is reasonably needed to provide the requested feature. AI output may be inaccurate and should be reviewed by the user.

5. Resumes, applications, and employer access

Candidates may be able to keep an uploaded resume and an Alygnn-built resume and choose which one Alygnn should use as the active resume. The active selection is used where the Service supports resume-based applications and employer access.

When a candidate applies to a job, the employer and authorized employer team members may be able to view candidate information and the resume made available through Alygnn. Employers may also be able to download the resume. Once information has been viewed, downloaded, or otherwise received by an employer, Alygnn cannot guarantee that changing the active resume or deleting the account will remove copies already retained by that employer.

Employers are independent users of the Service and are responsible for their own use, storage, disclosure, and deletion of candidate information after they receive it, subject to applicable law.

6. Messaging and notifications

Messages are stored so candidates and employers can communicate about an application. Conversation participants and authorized employer team members may have access to messages associated with their recruiting workflow. Messages are not intended to be public.

The current candidate messaging flow may require the employer to initiate the conversation after an application before the candidate can reply. We also store notification records and read/unread state for events such as new applicants, messages, and application activity.

We may send transactional emails or supported app/browser notifications for account verification, password resets, messages, applications, billing, security, and other Service-related events.

7. How information is shared

We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • With employers: when a candidate applies or otherwise uses a supported recruiting flow, the employer may receive application information, profile information, the applicable resume, and related job-specific information.
  • With employer team members: authorized administrators or recruiters may access candidate, job, message, and recruiting information available to their employer account.
  • With service providers: vendors process information for authentication, hosting, database, file storage, email delivery, payments, AI-assisted features, location/place services, security, and other infrastructure.
  • For legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, investigate fraud or abuse, respond to legal process, enforce our Terms, or protect users and the Service.
  • In a business transaction: information may be transferred in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not currently use personal information for third-party cross-context behavioral advertising.

8. Service providers

Depending on the feature used, Alygnn may rely on providers such as:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage, including resume and application-related data
  • Stripe — payment processing, subscriptions, invoices, billing events, and payment-status information
  • Resend or other email delivery providers — verification, password reset, transactional, and service emails
  • Google — Google sign-in and, where enabled, location/place services
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple
  • Vercel or other hosting/infrastructure providers — website and service hosting
  • AI model providers — resume parsing, matching support, job-description assistance, and content-review features

These providers process information under their own agreements, security practices, and privacy terms. Providers may change as Alygnn's infrastructure evolves.

9. Cookies, local storage, and similar technologies

We use cookies, local storage, session storage, and similar browser or app technologies to keep users signed in, preserve per-tab sessions, remember preferences and drafts, support resume-builder functionality, maintain onboarding state, and operate security and Service features.

We do not currently use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels for targeted advertising.

10. Data retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain account and application history, support billing and fraud prevention, resolve disputes, comply with law, enforce agreements, and protect the Service.

Retention periods may differ by data type. For example, payment records may be retained to satisfy accounting or legal requirements; employer verification records may be retained to document verification or fraud-prevention activity; and application or message records may be retained for legitimate recruiting, security, support, or dispute-resolution purposes.

You may request account deletion or use available account controls. Deletion from Alygnn does not necessarily delete copies already downloaded, exported, or lawfully retained by an employer or another independent recipient. Backup copies may persist for a limited period before deletion or overwrite.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including access controls and security features provided by our infrastructure vendors. No transmission or storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
