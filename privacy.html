1. Overview

Alygnn ("Alygnn," "we," "us," or "our") provides a job-matching and recruiting platform through alygnn.com, our Android app, and related services (together, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect information about candidates, employers, and employer team members.

This policy applies when you register or sign in, create a profile, upload a resume, browse or apply to jobs, post or manage jobs, communicate about applications, receive notifications, manage an employer account, or purchase a paid feature.

2. Information we collect

Account information

We collect account details such as your name, email address, account role, settings, identifiers, and verification status. Authentication credentials and supported Google or Apple sign-in tokens are handled through our authentication systems and providers; Alygnn does not need access to your plaintext password to operate your account.

If you sign in using Google or Apple, that provider may provide information such as your account identifier, email address, and basic profile details permitted by your sign-in choices. We use this information to authenticate and secure your account.

Job seeker information

  • Your resume file and any information extracted from it
  • Profile details such as your headline and photo, if you add one
  • Job preferences you set, including desired role, salary range, location, work style, required benefits, job type, and preferred industries
  • Your activity on the Service, such as jobs you apply to or skip
  • Application history and status (including applied, shortlisted/accepted, not selected, skipped and saved jobs), matching information, and activity timestamps
  • Messages exchanged with employers about applications and any attachments or details you voluntarily include

Employer information

  • Company name, description, website, phone number, business email, and business address
  • Verification status and any documentation or details submitted for business verification
  • Job postings you create, including title, description, requirements, benefits, salary, and location
  • Information about candidates who apply to your postings, to the extent needed to operate the Service
  • Business-verification identifiers and documents, employer team roles, account changes, job-slot use and candidate-review history

Payment, subscription, and billing information

When an employer makes a purchase through Alygnn, payment information is processed by Stripe or another payment processor. Our payment processor may securely retain the payment method for future Alygnn transactions, including subscription renewals, add-ons, one-time purchases, and other charges the employer separately authorizes.

Alygnn may receive and store limited payment-method and billing information made available by the payment processor, such as the card brand, last four digits, expiration date, customer or payment-method identifiers, transaction amount, payment status, subscription status, and billing history. Alygnn does not receive or store the full payment-card number or card security code.

Automatically collected information

We may collect device type, operating system and app version, browser information, IP address, approximate location derived from technical information, event times, diagnostic and security logs, and general Service usage needed to operate and troubleshoot Alygnn. If push notifications are enabled, we also process app/device push registration tokens and delivery-related information.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Match job seekers with relevant job postings based on stated preferences and resume content
  • Allow employers to post jobs, review applicants, and manage their hiring pipeline
  • Verify employer identity and business legitimacy before allowing job postings to go live
  • Send account-related communications, such as verification emails and application updates
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the Service, including detecting fraudulent or abusive activity
  • Improve and develop new features
  • Allow candidates and employers to exchange messages, and display conversation history and read/unread states
  • Deliver in-app, email, and—when permitted—device push notifications about applications, employer decisions, new messages, and account or security events
  • Manage employer subscriptions, billing, invoices, job slots, boosts, team seats, and account life-cycle requests

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. AI-powered features

Alygnn uses artificial intelligence, including third-party AI models, to support certain features, such as:

  • Parsing and interpreting resume content to help match job seekers with relevant postings
  • Generating draft job descriptions for employers based on details they provide
  • Reviewing custom requirement or benefit entries employers add to a job posting, to help ensure listings are meaningful and free of spam

When an AI-assisted feature is used, relevant resume or job-posting content may be processed by an AI service provider to produce the requested result. We aim to limit information shared to what is needed for that feature. AI output can be inaccurate and should be reviewed by the user; employers remain responsible for hiring decisions and content they publish.

5. Messages and notifications

After you apply for a job, an employer may start a conversation with you through Alygnn. We store the message content, conversation participants, associated job or application, timestamps, and read/unread status so conversations can appear in each participant’s account. Authorized employer team members may have access to messages related to their employer account. Messages are not public job listings.

Alygnn may send or display notifications for new messages, job applications, employer actions (such as shortlisting, hiring, or not selecting a candidate), business verification, password changes, account deactivation or reactivation, and other account or security events. We may store a notification’s type, relevant account or job identifier, timestamp, and read/unread or delivery state.

Push notifications: When supported and enabled on Android, Alygnn uses a device-specific push token to route notifications to your device. Tokens and delivery data may be processed through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and hosting/infrastructure systems that send the notification. Notification content can be visible in device alerts depending on your phone settings. You can manage app notifications through Alygnn’s available settings and your device’s notification permissions. Turning off device notifications does not necessarily remove messages or in-app notifications already stored in your account.

6. How information is shared

We share information in the following circumstances:

  • With authorized employer team members: when you apply, the employer’s authorized team members may review your application, candidate profile, and submitted resume in their hiring workspace.
  • To deliver notifications: limited device token and notification delivery data may be processed by push-notification delivery and hosting providers.
  • With employers: if you are a job seeker and apply to a job, the employer who posted that job can see the application details and resume information you choose to submit.
  • With service providers: we share information with vendors who help us operate the Service, as described in the "Third-party service providers" section below.
  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information if required to do so by law, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, investigate fraud, or ensure the safety of our users.
  • In a business transfer: if Alygnn is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

7. Third-party service providers

We rely on the following categories of third-party providers to operate the Service:

  • Supabase — account authentication, database, and file storage, including resumes, applications, and messages
  • Stripe — employer purchases, subscriptions, billing, invoices, receipts, and payment status
  • Google — supported sign-in services and Firebase Cloud Messaging for Android push delivery
  • Apple — supported Apple account authentication
  • AI service providers — applicable resume parsing, job-description assistance, matching support, or content review
  • Email delivery providers — confirmation, password reset, billing, account, and message-related emails
  • Website, API, and cloud infrastructure providers — hosting, reliable delivery, maintenance, and security

Each of these providers processes data on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) that are necessary to keep you signed in and to remember basic preferences. We do not currently use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

9. Data retention and account deletion

We retain information while your account is active and for as long as reasonably needed to operate the Service, provide recruiting and messaging functions, investigate security or fraud issues, resolve disputes, and satisfy legal or accounting requirements. Retention periods differ by data type; for example, limited invoices, payment and transaction records, security records, and verification records may be kept when legally required or otherwise necessary for legitimate reasons. Stored backups may take additional time to cycle out. An employer may separately retain a resume or application already received or downloaded, subject to the employer’s own legal responsibilities.

Deactivating an account

Deactivation is different from deletion. Where the option is available, deactivation temporarily disables access and may close associated employer job postings and cancel subscriptions. For employer accounts using Alygnn’s current deactivation feature, signing in again within 30 days can reactivate the account; accounts not reactivated during that period are scheduled for deletion. Deactivation itself is not an immediate deletion request.

Request account and associated data deletion

You can request deletion of your Alygnn account and associated personal data directly in the app through its available Settings or Account & Security options, choosing Delete Account rather than Deactivate Account. Account deletion is intended to be permanent and cannot be undone after it is completed.

Outside the app: You can also request deletion by emailing admin@alygnn.com from the email associated with your Alygnn account. Include the words “Delete my Alygnn account” so we can identify your request. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting; never email your password, full payment-card number, or government identification unless specifically requested through a secure verified process.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, receive a copy of, or delete personal information and to make other requests under applicable law. You can update many account details in Alygnn settings. To request deletion from outside the app, use the account-deletion instructions above or contact admin@alygnn.com.

12. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for users who are at least 18 years old, or who otherwise meet the legal working age in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

13. International users

Alygnn is based in the United States and currently focused on the U.S. job market. If you access the Service from outside the United States, your information will be processed and stored in the United States.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.

15. Contact us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, our handling of your information, or account and data deletion, email admin@alygnn.com. For deletion requests, see Request account and associated data deletion.
